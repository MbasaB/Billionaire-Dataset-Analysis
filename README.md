# 💰 Billionaires Dataset Analysis

An Excel-based data analysis project exploring the world's billionaires — their wealth, industries, countries, and demographics.

---

## 📌 Project Overview

This guided project involved downloading a real-world billionaires dataset, cleaning it, and analyzing it using Microsoft Excel. The goal was to uncover patterns and insights about the world's wealthiest individuals.

---

## 📂 Dataset

- **Source:** Public billionaires dataset
- **Records:** 475+ billionaires
- **Columns:** 24 (name, net worth, country, industry, age, gender, self-made status, and more)

---

## 🛠️ What I Did

### 1. 🧹 Data Cleaning
- Removed duplicates and handled missing values
- Calculated age from birth date
- Formatted date columns correctly
- Standardized column names and data types

### 2. 📊 Statistical Analysis
- Calculated descriptive statistics (mean, standard deviation, min/max) for:
  - Net worth (`finalWorth`)
  - Age
  - Country-level indicators (GDP, CPI, tax rate, population)

### 3. 🔄 Pivot Tables
- **Wealth by Industry** – which sectors produce the most billionaires
- **Wealth by Country** – top countries by total and average net worth
- **Age Group Breakdown** – distribution of billionaires by age range
- **Gender Analysis** – male vs female billionaire comparison
- **Self-Made vs Inherited** – proportion of self-made billionaires

### 4. 💡 Key Insights
- Elon Musk ranked #1 with a net worth of $180,000M
- The majority of billionaires are self-made
- Technology and Fashion & Retail are among the top industries
- The United States has the highest concentration of billionaires
- Average billionaire age is approximately 70 years
